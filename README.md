# 📊 Flipkart Retail Data Analysis | 80,000 Products | Real Business Insights

What really drives sales on an e-commerce platform like Flipkart?
I analyzed 80,000 retail product listings to uncover how pricing, discounts, ratings, delivery time, and seller performance impact sales.

## 🔍 Key Insights:
# ✅ Top-selling categories:
 Toys, Electronics, Appliances, Fashion
# ✅ Top brands by units sold:
 Nike, Reebok, Adidas, Puma, Apple
# ✅ Discounts work — but strategically
 Higher discounts reduce final price and generally increase units sold.
# ✅ Ratings & reviews matter
 Products with higher ratings and review counts consistently show better sales performance.
# ✅ Faster delivery = happier customers
 Lower delivery days are linked with improved ratings and sales.
# ✅ Seller rating influences success
 Better-rated sellers sell more — trust drives conversion.

## 📌 Business Takeaway:
Sales growth isn’t just about stock or return policy.
 👉 Pricing strategy, brand trust, seller reputation, and customer experience matter more.
